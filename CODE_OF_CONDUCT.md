# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or level of contribution.

## Our standards

Positive behaviour includes empathy, constructive feedback, respect for differing viewpoints, and accepting responsibility. Unacceptable behaviour includes harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, threats, and publishing private information without permission.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and interactions that violate this code. Report conduct concerns privately through the maintainer contact methods available on the GitHub profile. Reports will be reviewed fairly and kept confidential where possible.
